# Bioverse Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 October 2026_

This policy explains what the **Bioverse** Discord bot ("the bot", "we") does with your data. The bot only works inside the Bioverse Discord community server and its two helper servers (Appeals and Verify).

## What we collect

**When you register in the Bioverse server**, the bot saves the answers you give it on purpose:

- your Discord user ID
- your pronouns, birthday (to check you are 18+), region and the languages you speak
- the bio you write, and your avatar choices (skin tone, hair, clothing, expression)
- your registration progress, and whether staff accepted or rejected your bio

**While you use the server**, the bot saves:

- BioCoins, XP and level, and the items you bought in the wardrobe
- best friend bonds, greetings, favorites, waves, pokes and pats, along with the time limits on them
- flags, report tickets, punishments (timeouts, bans, quarantines) and appeals, so staff can moderate
- whether you are selfie or age-group verified, and when your verification threads were opened

## Message content

The bot reads messages in the Bioverse server for these purposes:

- registration answers that you type to the bot, and the word "agree"
- the word "verify" in the Verify server
- chat game answers in the chat channel
- best friend activity (two best friends chatting in the same minute)
- checking bios against the blocked-word list
- message logs for staff: when a message is edited or deleted, the old text is posted in a private staff log channel on Discord

**Normal chat messages are not stored by the bot.** They are only checked in memory at the moment they are sent. The only message text the bot saves is what you choose to give it in registration: your answers and your bio.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell or share your data with anyone outside the Bioverse staff team.
- We do not use your data or messages to train machine learning or AI models.
- We do not use online status / presence data.
- Selfie and age verification videos are posted to a private staff channel on Discord. The bot does not keep a copy.

## Where the data is kept

The data is kept in a private database run by the Bioverse team. Only the bot and the Bioverse developer can access it.

## How long we keep it

- **While you are a member:** we keep your data.
- **Moderation records:** punishments, flags and appeals may be kept after you leave, so the server stays safe.
- **Everything else:** deleted on request.

## Your choices

- You can choose not to register; nothing about you is saved in that case.
- You can edit your bio, and turn greetings off.
- You can ask staff (open a ticket in the Bioverse server) to see or delete your data.

## Contact

Open a ticket in the Bioverse Discord server, or contact the Bioverse staff team there.
