Bioverse bot

Bioverse is a private community bot for one 18+ social Discord server and its Appeals and Verify servers. These images are what the bot posts in the Bioverse Discord server. Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

Server Members intent

When a member joins, the bot starts their registration and gives roles step by step: pronoun roles, the avatar icon role and the member role after staff accept the bio. Member, role and nickname changes go to a private staff log channel. Member names and avatars appear on the bot's cards.

Message Content intent

Members type their registration answers to the bot in their private registration thread; the bot reads and checks them (date format, bio length, blocked words) and asks for confirmation. Chat games read answers in the chat channel. Best friend bonds count chatting at the same time.
