4. **Moderation.** Staff use the bot for flags, tickets, timeouts, bans, quarantines and appeals. Staff decisions can be appealed through the Appeals server.
5. **Availability.** The bot is provided as is. It may be changed, paused or stopped at any time.
6. **Privacy.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

Questions? Open a ticket in the Bioverse Discord server.
